Which perils sit over the most capacity

How to read this

Ratings are FEMA's National Risk Index (December 2025 release) for the county each campus sits in. NRI risk blends expected annual loss with social vulnerability and community resilience, so it describes the county, not the site, and is a screening tool, not a substitute for site modeling. "Inland flood" is NRI's inland flooding hazard (it replaced riverine flooding). Coastal flood is omitted: it is not applicable or very low for every county here. Watch the clusters: tornado, hail and wind across the Plains and Midwest, ice storm across Oklahoma, and the Memphis area's combination of tornado, flood and New Madrid earthquake.
